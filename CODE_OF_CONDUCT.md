# Code of conduct

This project welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels and
abilities. Be patient with beginners, explain technical disagreements respectfully,
and focus feedback on the work. English and German contributions are welcome.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable. Do not pressure others to reveal
personal details or to test unsafe wiring.

These rules apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other project
spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive
conversations and restrict participation. Responses should consider context and
the impact on the people affected.

If you see a problem, avoid escalating the exchange. Use GitHub's **Report content**
option on the relevant content for a private report to GitHub; availability depends
on the content type. Maintainers can also moderate public project discussions.
Do not post sensitive evidence or another person's details in a public issue.
For software vulnerabilities, use the separate [security reporting channel](SECURITY.md).

## Deutsch

Sei respektvoll und geduldig, auch mit Einsteigerinnen und Einsteigern. Kritik
gilt der Sache. Belästigung, Diskriminierung, Drohungen und die Veröffentlichung
privater Angaben werden nicht akzeptiert. Nutze bei Missbrauch die Meldefunktion
von GitHub und veröffentliche keine sensiblen Belege in einem öffentlichen Issue.
